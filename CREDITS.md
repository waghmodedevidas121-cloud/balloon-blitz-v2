## 🔊 Sound Design Architecture

Custom-synthesized audio effects generated via the Web Audio API without external binary dependencies:

- **Balloon Pops:** Dynamic multi-timbral acoustics with pentatonic pitch scaling that ascends with combo multipliers.
  - *Red (Classic):* Crisp snap with dual-frequency air burst impulse.
  - *Blue (Splash):* Water droplet pop with pitch bend resonance.
  - *Green (Woodland):* Snappy acoustic percussion pop with rich midrange body.
  - *Pink (Bubblegum):* Elastic double-burst squish pop.
  - *Gold (Treasury):* Triple-chime metallic sparkle with harmonic shimmer and coin cascade.
  - *Freeze (Cryo):* Sub-zero crystal crunch and icy bell cascade.
  - *Bomb (Volcanic):* Multi-oscillator cinematic sub-bass explosion (140Hz down to 28Hz) with filtered brown-noise rumble.
  - *Gift (Wonder):* Ascending major triad magic powerup chime.
- **Slingshot Acoustics:**
  - *Rubber Draw:* Pitch-tracked tension stretching warble.
  - *Snap:* High-velocity leather release and whistling projectile flight whoosh.
  - *Wall Ricochet:* Natural wooden bank ping with randomized micro-tuning.
- **Boss Combat:**
  - *Boss Armor Impact:* Metallic armor thud with sparks.
  - *Boss Defeat:* Three-stage cascading detonation and grand victory fanfare.
- **User Interface:**
  - *Tactile Tap:* High-end wooden micro-transient.
  - *Coin & Gem Pickups:* Arcade twin-chime ringers (B5 to E6).
  - *Victory Jingle:* Uplifting polyphonic brass fanfare.
  - *Life Lost / Defeat:* Descending minor buzz with low-frequency thump.
